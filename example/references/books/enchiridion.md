## BookReview: My Review

@rating: ★★★
@read_date: 2025-10-20

The Enchiridion, or "Handbook," is Epictetus' distilled wisdom on living the Stoic life. This compact manual is remarkably accessible and immediately practical, making ancient philosophy relevant to modern life.

What distinguishes this work is its directness. Epictetus doesn't philosophize about what might be - he tells you what is and what you must do about it. The famous dichotomy of control that opens the work provides a framework that, if truly internalized, can transform how we approach every challenge.

The book's brevity is its strength. You can read it in an hour, but understanding and applying its principles is the work of a lifetime. Essential reading for anyone seeking mental clarity and resilience.
