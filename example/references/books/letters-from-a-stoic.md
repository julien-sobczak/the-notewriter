## BookReview: My Review

`@read_date: 2025-09-05`

Seneca's letters to his friend Lucilius offer a more personal and intimate view of Stoicism than most ancient texts. Written in conversational style, these essays cover everything from time management to facing death, always with practical wisdom.

What sets these letters apart is Seneca's warmth and humanity. He admits his own struggles and imperfections, making his philosophy more relatable. His insights on time management, particularly in "On the Shortness of Life," remain startlingly relevant today.

The letter format makes this book highly accessible - you can read one letter at a time, reflect on it, and return when ready. This is Stoicism at its most applicable and compassionate.
