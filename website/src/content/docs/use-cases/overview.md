title: Overview

Notes are powerful. You can use notes for almost anything. In this part, we will cover the main use cases and how _The NoteWriter_ supports them.

* Discover how to organize your [reading notes](./reading.md) inside your Second Brain and how to create a commonplace book.
* Discover how to study your [flashcards](./learning.md) and remember (almost) anything you want to store in your long-term memory.
* Discover how to create your [journal](./journaling.md) and how to integrate new routines in your workflow to practice self-reflection.
* Discover how to track progress on your [projects](./planning.md) and find your next task.
* Discover how to [write](./writing.md) and stop being afraid of the blank page and overcome the writer's block.
* Discover how to use notes to tackle any [creative project](./creating.md) by rendering notes like your would do on a physical desk.
