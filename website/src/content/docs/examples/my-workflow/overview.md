I use notes in different contexts.

* I collect notes on a [daily-basis](./daily.md) when a thought, an non-urgent task or an idea popup in my mind. These notes are usually captured outside _The NoteWriter_. I processed them during my weekly review.
* I collect notes while [reading](./reading.md) books. I usually processed them after completing a book so that my annotations are still fresh in mind. I create many flashcards during that process and I sometimes publish a book review on my blog using a custom hook.
* I use a project for every [writing](./writing.md). I slowly collect seeds to not start with a blank page and I use a desk template to render them to navigate easily between ideas when writing.
