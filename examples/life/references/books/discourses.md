## BookReview: My Review

`@rating: ★★★`
`@read_date: 2025-12-10`

The Discourses provide a more extensive exploration of Epictetus' philosophy than the Enchiridion. These recorded lectures offer deep insights into Stoic practice, covering topics from dealing with difficult people to understanding the nature of good and evil.

What makes the Discourses valuable is seeing philosophy in action - watching a teacher respond to students, using examples and dialogues to illuminate principles. The conversational style makes complex ideas more accessible.

This is a book to return to repeatedly. Each reading reveals new layers of meaning and practical wisdom. While more demanding than the Enchiridion, the effort is well rewarded.
