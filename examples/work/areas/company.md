# Acme Corporation

## Reference: History

`@source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acme_Corporation`

**Acme Corporation** is a _fictional company_ featured in numerous Warner Bros. cartoons, particularly the _Road Runner and Wile E. Coyote_ series. Founded in the **early 1900s**, Acme specializes in manufacturing a wide variety of products, from simple household items to complex machinery and explosives. The company is known for its catalog of _innovative yet often unreliable_ products that frequently malfunction in comical ways. Despite its fictional nature, Acme has become an iconic symbol in popular culture, representing the quintessential mail-order company and serving as a placeholder name for generic corporations in various media.
