# Acme Corporation

## Reference: History

Acme Corporation is a fictional company featured in numerous Warner Bros. cartoons, particularly the Road Runner and Wile E. Coyote series. Founded in the early 1900s, Acme specializes in manufacturing a wide variety of products, from simple household items to complex machinery and explosives. The company is known for its catalog of innovative yet often unreliable products that frequently malfunction in comical ways. Despite its fictional nature, Acme has become an iconic symbol in popular culture, representing the quintessential mail-order company and serving as a placeholder name for generic corporations in various media.
